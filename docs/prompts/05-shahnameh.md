# Set 5 — Shahnameh Hero Backgrounds (شاهنامه)

**Format:** 2560×1440 (16:9), full-bleed Persian miniature — same flattened
perspective / scalloped hills / gold accents family as the Hafez set. Each
card adds ONE hero rendered as a **bold flat silhouette in deep indigo
#232A4A**, placed entirely within the RIGHT third, reading instantly as a
shape with no facial detail. LEFT 40% stays calm for overlaid text. No text,
no watermark. Sunset-band skies shared across the set for cohesion.

## 1. Rostam — `public/results/bg/shahnameh-rostam.png`
Silhouette: broad-shouldered hero standing planted, ox-headed mace resting
over one shoulder, leopard-spot texture hinted in slightly lighter indigo,
cloak hanging heavy. Scene: mountain pass with layered rust-and-violet peaks,
a winding road below, enormous sunset sky in apricot bands with a low gold
sun. Two tiny distant travelers sheltered behind his heels for scale.

## 2. Gordafarid — `public/results/bg/shahnameh-gordafarid.png`
Silhouette: armored heroine atop a rearing horse, plume streaming, bow drawn
and aimed skyward, cape whipping. Scene: a crenellated fortress tower rising
behind her from the right edge, dawn sky in rose-gold bands, first light
catching the battlements, a few arrow shapes arcing harmlessly away.
Left: open rampart walk and calm sky.

## 3. Siyavash — `public/results/bg/shahnameh-siyavash.png`
Silhouette: perfectly upright knight beside his horse, both utterly still;
beside them a single tall narrow flame — the trial-by-fire motif — drawn in
warm gold-orange against the cool figure. Scene: serene symmetrical courtyard,
ivory-to-gold zenith sky, two slim cypresses mirroring each other, gold dust
motes rising. The calmest card of the set.

## 4. Sohrab — `public/results/bg/shahnameh-sohrab.png`
Silhouette: young warrior mid-charge leaning hard into the wind, cloak and
plume streaming horizontally backward, sword trailing. Scene: storm clouds
splitting above with lightning bolts shaped like falling arrows, hot orange
core glowing inside dark indigo clouds, ground line tilting with speed, loose
rocks lifting off. The most kinetic card of the set.

## 5. Epic Ensemble (balanced) — `public/results/bg/shahnameh-ensemble.png`
Four smaller silhouettes together on a hilltop ridge in the right half:
mace-bearer, mounted archer, flame-bearer, charging storm-bearer — distinct
poses, evenly spaced. Scene: vast epic valley below with rivers like gold
ribbon, and the mythical simurgh bird circling once in the upper sky trailing
gold stars. Dusk sky in deep rose and indigo bands.
