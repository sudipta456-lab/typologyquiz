# Set 1 — Kkotmal Flower Icons (꽃말)

**Format:** 1024×1024 PNG, transparent background. One flower motif, centered,
≥10% padding. Flat vector, soft solid fills, light two-tone shading allowed on
petals only. Identical rendering rules for all five icons so they read as a
family. Used small (result header, thumbnails), so silhouettes must stay
readable at 48px.

Shared prompt prefix for every icon:
`Minimalist flat vector flower icon, no outlines, soft solid colors, transparent background, centered with generous margins.`

## 1. Forsythia — `public/results/icons/kkotmal-forsythia.png`
A graceful arching forsythia spray: one slender olive-green stem curving from
lower-left to upper-right, studded with seven to nine small four-petaled
trumpet flowers in warm golden yellow #F6C445 with deeper orange #E8A33D
centers, a few tiny bright-green new leaf buds along the stem. Cheerful,
upward-moving composition.

## 2. Plum Blossom — `public/results/icons/kkotmal-plum.png`
A cluster of three five-petaled plum blossoms on a short knobby dark-brown
branch segment entering from the lower-right corner. Round overlapping petals
in blush pink #F2C4CE fading to white at the edges, clusters of thin stamens
tipped with tiny deep-red dots, one delicate snowflake resting on the top
blossom. Quiet, wintry elegance.

## 3. Camellia — `public/results/icons/kkotmal-camellia.png`
One fully opened rose-form camellia seen slightly from above: many layers of
rounded overlapping petals, deep red #C94F5B on the outer rings shifting to
rich crimson #A03242 toward the tight spiral center, two glossy oval
dark-green leaves (#2E5641) beneath, one small round bud on a short stem.
Dense, luxurious, devoted.

## 4. Cosmos — `public/results/icons/kkotmal-cosmos.png`
A single cosmos flower on a thin, gently S-curved green stem: eight wide
rounded petals with softly notched tips, soft pink #F2A7BC lightening toward
the petal edges, a small button of golden-yellow filaments at the center,
wispy thread-like leaves branching off the stem. Airy, weightless, calm.

## 5. Wildflower Mix (balanced) — `public/results/icons/kkotmal-mix.png`
A small loose round posy combining all four: a forsythia spray, one plum
blossom, one mini camellia, and one cosmos, tied together with a single thin
cream ribbon loop at the base. Balanced circular arrangement, all four
palettes present but none dominating.
