# Set 3 — Fal-e Hafez Persian-Miniature Backgrounds (فال حافظ)

**Format:** 2560×1440 (16:9), full-bleed. NO characters, NO text, NO frames.
Persian miniature painting style: flattened stacked perspective, scalloped
stylized hills, slim cypress trees, small gold ornamental accents, jewel tones
over aged-paper warmth. Keep the LEFT 40% of each composition calm and
low-detail — result text overlays there. One consistent sky treatment across
all cards so they read as pages of the same book.

## 1. Nightingale — `public/results/bg/hafez-nightingale.png`
Dawn rose garden: rose bushes heavy with pink blooms climbing a low turquoise
wall on the right, petals drifting upward, one slim dark cypress, scalloped
hills behind, banded sunrise sky in apricot and rose with a small gold sun
disc. A few musical-note-shaped petals rise where a nightingale would sing.
Left third: quiet pale wall and soft ground plane for text.

## 2. Sage — `public/results/bg/hafez-sage.png`
Still midnight courtyard: tall dark cypresses framing the right edge, a
perfectly calm reflecting pool holding a crisp full-moon reflection, one
distant palace facade with a single lit turquoise window, deep lapis sky
#1F3A5F scattered with thin gold stars. Absolute serenity, symmetrical,
unhurried. Left third: smooth water and plain courtyard tiles.

## 3. Rebel — `public/results/bg/hafez-rebel.png`
An ornate brick garden wall crossing the right half, flowering branches
bursting through its cracks; an elegant jug tipped on its side spilling a
cascade of roses instead of wine; one glowing lantern leaning defiantly into
the wind; petals blowing hard sideways; tilted dusk sky in burnt orange and
violet. Left third: calmer open sky and ground.

## 4. Cupbearer — `public/results/bg/hafez-cupbearer.png`
Warm evening celebration: turquoise-domed pavilion with slender columns on
the right, strings of glowing gold lanterns swooping overhead, a low spread of
cushions with pomegranates, teacups and a steaming samovar, gentle steam
curls, festive but orderly. Rose-and-gold evening sky. Left third: soft carpet
plane and open air.

## 5. Open Page (balanced) — `public/results/bg/hafez-openpage.png`
A large open illuminated manuscript lying across the lower right, gold
illuminated margins, with all four scenes rising gently from its pages as
flat paper-cut layers: rose garden, moonlit pool, cracked wall, lantern
pavilion — arranged like a diorama skyline. Tiny gold stars above. Left
third: plain aged-paper tone with a faint ghost arabesque pattern.
