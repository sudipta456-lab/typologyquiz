# Set 2 — Doubutsu Uranai Animal Portraits (動物占い)

**Format:** 1200×1600 px (3:4 portrait). Single animal, chest-up or seated
full-body, facing the viewer. Flat vector, no outlines, soft two-tone shading,
large expressive eyes. Behind each animal: exactly one big geometric shape
(circle / arch / crescent) as a halo plus two small floating accent motifs —
this keeps the set consistent. Plain flat backdrop fills the rest. No text,
no border frames.

## 1. The Shiba — `public/results/doubutsu-shiba.png`
Cheerful shiba inu portrait sitting bolt upright, facing forward, cream and
warm-orange coat #E8853B with white muzzle and chest, characteristic curled
tail peeking past its side. Eyes closed in happy upside-down-U arcs, open
smiling mouth with a small tongue tip. Big pale-gold sun circle #F4D9A8
directly behind its head; two golden ginkgo leaves drifting beside it; soft
warm-beige #FFF6EA backdrop.

## 2. The Cat — `public/results/doubutsu-cat.png`
Elegant black-and-white tuxedo cat seated neatly, tail wrapped around its
front paws, chin slightly lifted. Half-lidded jade-green eyes, serene, faintly
aloof expression, white muzzle marking. Slim gold crescent moon behind its
head; two tiny stars and one drifting cherry petal around it; deep indigo
night-blue #2C3550 backdrop.

## 3. The Owl — `public/results/doubutsu-owl.png`
Round watchful owl perched on a small pine sprig, front-facing, plumage in
layered blues #3E5878 with cream speckles, tiny ear tufts, small amber beak.
Enormous circular amber-gold eyes with calm confident pupils. Huge pale
full-moon disc #EFE3C2 directly behind it; three tiny stars and two fireflies
floating nearby; darkest navy #16213A backdrop of the set.

## 4. The Kitsune — `public/results/doubutsu-kitsune.png`
Sly white-faced kitsune with orange mantle #E88C3A, black-tipped ears and
paws, sitting with poise; two to three fluffy tails fanned behind it. Knowing
closed-eye smile, one eye opening just a sliver with a glinting amber iris.
Small vermilion torii-gate silhouette behind its head; two floating foxfire
flames in teal #2FA8A0 and gold #F2C94C; dusky purple #4A3B57 backdrop.

## 5. Shapeshifter Spirit (balanced) — `public/results/doubutsu-spirit.png`
A friendly mythical creature combining the set: shiba ears and curled orange
tail, tuxedo-cat half-lidded green eyes, small blue owl wings folded at its
back, one extra fluffy kitsune tail. Gentle knowing smile. Soft pearl-grey
circle behind it holding a faint moon-and-sun eclipse motif; a ginkgo leaf,
star, foxfire flame and cherry petal orbiting it; muted twilight mauve
backdrop.
