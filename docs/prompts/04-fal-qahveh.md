# Set 4 — Fal-e Qahveh Persian-Miniature Backgrounds (فال قهوه)

**Format:** 2560×1440 (16:9), full-bleed. NO characters, NO text, NO frames.
Same Persian miniature family as the Hafez set: flattened stacked perspective,
scalloped hills, slim cypresses, gold ornamental accents, jewel tones over
aged-paper warmth — but each card is built around ONE tasseography symbol
appearing large and unmistakable. Keep the LEFT 40% calm for text overlay.
Shared warm coffee-and-saffron undertone across the set so all four read as
one cup.

## 1. Mountain — `public/results/bg/fal-qahveh-mountain.png`
A bold triangular mountain range dominating the right half, layered flat
ridges in espresso brown #3E2A1E and umber #6B4A32 with cream snow caps, a
tiny gold flag on the highest peak, a narrow climbing path switchbacking up.
Foreground: a small copper coffee cup on a saucer resting on a patterned
carpet corner. Sky: warm banded dawn in caramel and cream. Left third: soft
plain carpet plane and pale sky.

## 2. Bird — `public/results/bg/fal-qahveh-bird.png`
A flock of stylized miniature birds sweeping across the upper right in one
graceful curve, wings in turquoise #2FA8A0 and gold, trailing thin swirling
ground-pattern ribbons behind them like stirred crema; one slim cypress below;
open sky filling most of the frame in warm cream and apricot bands. A sense
of news arriving and movement beginning. Left third: calm open sky.

## 3. Path — `public/results/bg/fal-qahveh-path.png`
A winding golden path curling from the lower left into scalloped hills on the
right, passing a tiny arched footbridge and three dotted waypoints marked with
small gold medallions; cypress pairs flanking the route; distant domed city on
the horizon; late-afternoon sky in saffron and rose bands. Left third: the
path's quiet opening stretch through plain ground.

## 4. Fish — `public/results/bg/fal-qahveh-fish.png`
A single graceful fish swimming upstream inside a stylized turquoise stream
that curves through the right half, its scales picked out in gold, three small
bubble rings around it; banks with slim cypresses and scattered wildflowers;
gentle current swirls suggesting fortune flowing inward. Warm cream sky above.
Left third: smooth bank and calm water edge.

## 5. Full Cup (balanced) — `public/results/bg/fal-qahveh-fullcup.png`
An oversized top-down view of an ornate copper coffee cup occupying the right
half, its dark crema surface crowded with many small ground symbols at once —
tiny mountain, bird, winding path, fish, crescent moon and four-pointed star
— each rendered in slightly lighter brown with delicate gold outlines; a thin
Persian tile pattern band around the inner rim; saucer edge with two sugar
cubes. Left third: plain carpet and table edge in warm neutral tone.
