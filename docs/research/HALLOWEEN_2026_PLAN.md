## Evidence and positioning

This is a seasonal editorial experiment, not a claim of proven North American engagement. The October 21 growth scorecard recorded one Google-organic interactive start with audience/QA limits; it does not identify demand for Halloween. The October 22 scout's library-friendly, supplied-evidence format informs the self-contained story and logic rounds. NASA's current October material supplies a timely science angle. The other primary sources establish factual research leads, not search-volume or ranking evidence.

Primary leads checked October 22:

- **NASA:** [October 2026 skywatching](https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/skywatching/whats-up-october-2026-skywatching-tips-from-nasa/) and [Spooky Stargazing](https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/skywatching/night-sky-network/spooky-stargazing/). Recheck date, location and visibility wording at authoring; a past October event must not be presented as occurring tonight.
- **BATS:** [Smithsonian Bat Facts](https://www.si.edu/spotlight/bats/batfacts). Source each species/behavior claim individually; do not generalize all bats from one species.
- **LOC:** [Halloween research guide](https://guides.loc.gov/halloween), [traditions](https://blogs.loc.gov/headlinesandheroes/2021/10/the-origins-of-halloween-traditions/) and [October 31 collections](https://www.loc.gov/item/today-in-history/october-31/). The guide also discusses Día de Muertos, a different observance; do not collapse the two. Individual catalog records and rights statements need inspection before reuse. Write original cases and paraphrases; no assumption that an archive makes an item public domain.
- **FDA:** [Nutrition Facts label](https://www.fda.gov/food/nutrition-food-labeling-and-critical-foods/changes-nutrition-facts-label). Label-reading and supplied-number arithmetic only, without dietary advice or food-safety assurances. Clearly identify US label conventions; do not imply Canadian labels use identical rules.
- **NOAA:** [Bioluminescence](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/biolum.html). Distinguish light made by organisms from reflected light/fluorescence using supporting references.
- **BOTANY:** [Illinois Extension pumpkin growing](https://extension.illinois.edu/gardening/pumpkin) and [pumpkins, gourds and squash](https://extension.illinois.edu/blogs/flowers-fruits-and-frass/2023-10-27-pumpkins-gourds-and-squash-oh-my). Verify each botanical distinction; avoid unsupported species-level keys.
- **ORIGINAL:** All data, maps, rules, rhythms and situations supplied by the author and clearly labelled fictional/original. This is a provenance label, not an external fact citation. Generalized real-world claims still need references.
