# Movie quizzes and music excerpts — October 15, 2026

## Release decision

Build original movie/character questions, commentary and visual clues. Do not copy competitor questions, poster compositions, film stills, actor portraits, dialogue or soundtracks. Use movie/character names to identify the subject; make no affiliation or endorsement claim. No IMDb images or commercial music recordings are authorized by this research. This is a practical implementation decision, not a legal opinion clearing every possible use.

## Studio copy and quiz wording

Studio and franchise synopsis text is copyrighted. Quiz explanations paraphrase the official pages in original wording and quote only short phrases (for example a single descriptive word) with the source link beside them. Each question records the page it relies on and the terms its claim needs; the source check is a keyword test, not proof of meaning, so a human still reads the claim against the page. No dialogue, famous lines or competitor questions are copied. Where a fact is not on the cited page (for example a director or release detail), it is left out rather than sourced loosely. Character-note questions use the "The Characters" sections of the official Disney pages as fetched on October 15, 2026.

## IMDb posters

[IMDb's current conditions](https://www.imdb.com/conditions/) identify images as copyrighted IMDb/supplier content. License and Site Access grants personal/non-commercial access and excludes commercial reproduction without express written consent. A poster being downloadable, publicly visible, promotional, credited, or small does not itself give our ad-supported website a reuse license. Hotlinking or cropping does not establish permission. An IMDb data/API subscription also must not be assumed to clear underlying poster artwork. A future poster release needs documented permission for each relevant image, commercial web display, territory, duration, permitted modifications and required credit. Fair use/fair dealing is contextual; no blanket poster clearance is asserted here.

## Thirty-second music quiz

Technically feasible, but **30 seconds is not an automatic copyright exception**. The [U.S. Copyright Office FAQ](https://www.copyright.gov/help/faq/faq-fairuse.html) says there is no fixed number of musical notes or percentage that can be safely taken; the whole use matters. Its [musician guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/engage/musicians/) and [music-income guide](https://www.copyright.gov/music-modernization/educational-materials/musicians-income.pdf) distinguish the underlying composition from the recording. A recording license does not automatically cover the composition, and a public-domain composition does not automatically make a modern recording public domain.

For Canada, [CIPO's infringement guidance](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canadian-intellectual-property-office/en/copyright-infringement) describes communicating recordings without permission as a potential infringement. [SOCAN's FAQ](https://www.socan.com/frequently-asked-questions/) distinguishes private listening from public use; [SOCAN licensing](https://www.socan.com/music-licensees/) is a starting point for identifying relevant performance/reproduction permissions, not proof one license covers all rights or countries. A personal Spotify/Apple Music subscription or TikTok music permission must not be treated as a license for an independent web quiz.

Safe starting options: (1) original commissioned tracks with the written rights needed for commercial quiz playback; (2) specifically licensed catalog excerpts covering both composition and recording, interactive quiz use and intended territories; (3) individually verified public-domain compositions **and** recordings, or our own recording of a composition verified public domain in target territories; (4) CC0 or commercial-use Creative Commons material with compatible conditions and complete attribution. Do not assume every royalty-free library permits a music-identification game; some restrict standalone music, guessing games or redistribution. Official provider previews/embeds need a separate terms check; a 30-second preview URL is not general authorization to copy/host it.

A later audio player should use explicit tap-to-play, a visible duration/progress indicator, pause/replay, keyboard controls and a non-audio alternative. Store a per-track rights manifest and stop publishing a clip when its license expires. Do not enable a commercial-song bank until its permissions are documented. No licenses purchased, rights holders contacted or audio clips uploaded in this task.

## Competitor observation

The public [Sporcle movie hub](https://www.sporcle.com/games/category/movies) was inspected through the connected Chrome UI on October 15. Visible examples included Title Character Movies by Picture, Movie Characters by Quote II, Pixar Reveal, Pixar Movies: Oldest to Newest, Movie Posters and Movie Word Quadrants VI. These support inspiration from **formats** (recognition, clues, ordering, short rounds), not copying their question wording, artwork or answer banks. No assertion of outperforming Sporcle or its users' preferences is made from this observation.

Our design choices are hypotheses to test: clear mobile choices, original funny but precise clues, deeper explanations, a complete missed-answer review, obvious spoiler notice, and a shared group board with QR joining. Actual completion, repeat play and sharing data are required to assess whether these help.
