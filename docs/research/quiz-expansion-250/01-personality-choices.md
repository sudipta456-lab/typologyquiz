Proposed concepts, October 18, 2026. All require newly authored question banks and separate slugs. Seeds illustrate tasks; they are not fact-checked answer keys. ## PC-01 · The Unplanned Saturday

**Wave A · Type: preference · evergreen · Build: preference**

Your plans just cancelled and the day is blank. See what your first moves say about how you spend open time.

**Rounds:** 8 scenarios: pick your Saturday move from two options → 2 rankings: order ideal Saturday blocks.

**Example task:** Your plans cancel. Do you invent a project of your own, or join a ready-made invitation? Later, choose one longer activity or several small samples.

**Result:** Open-day card: Self-Started vs Invitation-Led and One Commitment vs Small Samples; based only on these choices.

**Audience:** Adults and teens; North America; globally playable. **Proposed slug:** `unplanned-saturday`.

**Evidence:** [E00](SOURCES.md).

**Production:** Needs balanced pair authoring so neither pole sounds better. Review wording for culture-neutral weekends, shift-work households and accessibility. Playful, not diagnostic. Requires new preference scoring; never use correct/incorrect credit for taste choices. Author balanced items specifically for these named dimensions; do not reuse generic Plan/Improvise or Solo/Shared vectors.

**Wave B · Type: preference · experimental · Build: preference**

**Evidence:** [X00](SOURCES.md).
