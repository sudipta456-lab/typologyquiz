# Movies & TV: 25 new quizzes

[Catalog index](README.md) · [Delivery plan](../QUIZ_EXPANSION_250_PLAN_2026-10-18.md) · [Evidence ledger](SOURCES.md)

Proposed concepts, October 18, 2026. All require newly authored question banks and separate slugs. Seeds illustrate tasks; they are not fact-checked answer keys. Wave A = category pilot; B = next four; C = next ten; D = final ten. Dependencies override wave priority. Most target 3–6 minutes; actual durations require playtesting. Research references support rationale, not factual answers.

## MT-01 · Continuity Detective

**Wave B · Type: knowledge · evergreen · Build: sequence**

Spot the mismatched detail in original written scene notes and learn how crews keep a shoot consistent.

**Rounds:** 4 scene notes: choose the detail that breaks continuity → 3 cases: pick which crew role should have caught it → 3 shoot-day lists: order takes so the scene stays consistent.

**Example task:** Scene note: Mia pours coffee into a full mug, then later lifts it empty without drinking. Which detail breaks continuity?

**Result:** Raw score out of 10 with a short explanation of each continuity trap.

**Audience:** Film fans and curious casual viewers, all ages. **Proposed slug:** `continuity-detective-film-sets`.

**Evidence:** [E00](SOURCES.md).

**Production:** All scenes are original fictional notes. Source crew-role definitions from published industry references and have a working film professional review answers.
