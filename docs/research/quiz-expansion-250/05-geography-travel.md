# Geography & Travel: 25 new quizzes

[Catalog index](README.md) · [Delivery plan](../QUIZ_EXPANSION_250_PLAN_2026-10-18.md) · [Evidence ledger](SOURCES.md)

Proposed concepts, October 18, 2026. All require newly authored question banks and separate slugs. Seeds illustrate tasks; they are not fact-checked answer keys. Wave A = category pilot; B = next four; C = next ten; D = final ten. Dependencies override wave priority. Most target 3–6 minutes; actual durations require playtesting. Research references support rationale, not factual answers.

## GT-01 · Rain Shadow Road Trip

**Wave B · Type: knowledge · evergreen · Build: base**

Plan a drive across mountains and predict which stops get the rain before the weather decides your picnic.

**Rounds:** 4 scenarios: choose the likelier drier stop → 4 route legs: order expected rainfall using the supplied values and wind direction → 3 pairs: match landform to its weather effect.

**Example task:** Moist Pacific air crosses a mountain range heading east. Which stop is likelier drier: a town on the windward slope or one just east of the crest?

**Result:** Raw score out of 11 plus a short rain-shadow explanation for each miss; no percentile.

**Audience:** Road-trippers and students in Canada, Mexico, and the US. **Proposed slug:** `rain-shadow-road-trip`.

**Evidence:** [E00](SOURCES.md).

**Production:** Source every example from cited climatology references; confirm named ranges and prevailing winds. Describe each route in text so no map is needed. Use an explicitly simplified scenario; do not assume real places follow a universal wet-to-dry ranking.

## GT-02 · City-Region-Country Relay

**Wave A · Type: knowledge · trend-adjacent · Build: layers**

Pass each city to its province, state, or estado, then to its country, and watch several regions funnel into one.

**Rounds:** 4 cities: match each to its province, state, or estado → 4 regions: sort into Canada, Mexico, or US (many-to-one) → 3 chains: complete the city-region-country relay.

**Example task:** Match e.g. Monterrey to its estado, then place that estado with other regions that share its country. Which relay link is missing?

**Result:** Relay score by layer (city-to-region, region-to-country, full chain), with each broken link explained.

**Audience:** North American geography fans, teachers, and cross-border families. **Proposed slug:** `city-region-country-relay`.

**Evidence:** [R14](SOURCES.md).

**Production:** Verify every link against official government sources. Balance Canadian, Mexican, and US items. Text lists serve as the accessible alternative. French and Spanish editions need human adaptation.

**Evidence:** [E00](SOURCES.md).

**Production:** Verify offsets against the IANA tz database for each fixed date. Check Canadian, US, and Mexican DST rules and exceptions. Never use a live clock. Supply date-specific UTC offsets in the puzzle; verify them independently before publication.

**Evidence:** [E00](SOURCES.md).

**Production:** Verify each drainage path against hydrological sources, including endorheic and disputed-outlet cases. Provide text-only alternatives for any basin diagram. Treat a gulf and its parent ocean as different layers, never mutually exclusive peer buckets; state hydrological conventions. Document a regional naming policy and include recognized water-body aliases; names must never change the underlying hydrological scoring.
