## Research decisions

Prefer category breadth over squeezing all 250 ideas out of a few trending franchises. Current sources strongly support some themes and give little direct evidence for others. An original logic mechanic or historical object puzzle can still merit a test when honestly labeled evergreen or experimental.

Do not sum incomparable engagement metrics: viewers, searches, cumulative posts, survey percentages and daily return rates have different denominators. No source establishes high engagement for all 250 proposed experiences. No reliable recent, country-comparable completion/share dataset for independent quiz sites was established in this review.

The earlier [strategy report](../INTERACTIVE_QUIZ_STRATEGY_2026-10-18.md) adds research on forced-choice assessment, media rights, accessibility, fandom and earlier seasonal interest. Its constraints carry forward; no unchanged existing quiz is part of this expansion.
