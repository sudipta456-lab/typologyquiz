# Sports & Games: 25 new quizzes

[Catalog index](README.md) · [Delivery plan](../QUIZ_EXPANSION_250_PLAN_2026-10-18.md) · [Evidence ledger](SOURCES.md)

Proposed concepts, October 18, 2026. All require newly authored question banks and separate slugs. Seeds illustrate tasks; they are not fact-checked answer keys. Wave A = category pilot; B = next four; C = next ten; D = final ten. Dependencies override wave priority. Most target 3–6 minutes; actual durations require playtesting. Research references support rationale, not factual answers.

## SG-01 · Pick Your Co-op Role

**Wave A · Type: preference · experimental · Build: preference**

Find the role you naturally take when a team game turns chaotic, with no role ranked above another.

**Rounds:** 6 co-op scenarios: select the move you would make → 2 short rankings: prioritize scouting, support and coordination.

**Example task:** Your fictional squad splits and one teammate is lost. Do you scout ahead, regroup, help them reconnect, or guard the exit?

**Result:** Co-op choices card: Information vs Coordination and Prevention vs Recovery; no skill or team-suitability claim.

**Audience:** Co-op video game and tabletop players; North America; globally playable. **Proposed slug:** `co-op-role-picker`.

**Evidence:** [X00](SOURCES.md).

**Production:** Original scenarios across video, tabletop and team-sport settings. Review result wording so no role reads as best. Avoid franchise names. Requires new preference scoring; never use correct/incorrect credit for taste choices. Author balanced items specifically for these named dimensions; do not reuse generic Plan/Improvise or Solo/Shared vectors.
