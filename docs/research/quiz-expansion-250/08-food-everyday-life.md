# Food & Everyday Life: 25 new quizzes

[Catalog index](README.md) · [Delivery plan](../QUIZ_EXPANSION_250_PLAN_2026-10-18.md) · [Evidence ledger](SOURCES.md)

Proposed concepts, October 18, 2026. All require newly authored question banks and separate slugs. Seeds illustrate tasks; they are not fact-checked answer keys. Wave A = category pilot; B = next four; C = next ten; D = final ten. Dependencies override wave priority. Most target 3–6 minutes; actual durations require playtesting. Research references support rationale, not factual answers.

## FE-01 · Build a Home Cafe

**Wave A · Type: preference · trend-adjacent · Build: preference**

Furnish an imaginary corner cafe one small decision at a time and discover what kind of host you would be.

**Rounds:** 6 cafe scenarios: choose layout, menu and atmosphere details → 2 rankings: prioritize comfort, experimentation and hosting.

**Example task:** Your tiny cafe has one free wall: a chalkboard menu, a shelf of mismatched mugs, or a record player?

**Result:** A home-cafe card: Familiar vs Experimental and Private Retreat vs Shared Hangout.

**Audience:** Home-drink fans and cafe daydreamers; North America, globally playable. **Proposed slug:** `build-a-home-cafe`.

**Evidence:** [R07](SOURCES.md).

**Production:** Write original, price-free options; check every option maps to both dimensions; no drink-health claims; do not reuse report wording. Requires new preference scoring; never use correct/incorrect credit for taste choices.

## FE-02 · Kitchen Tool Detective

**Wave B · Type: knowledge · evergreen · Build: base**

Read the evidence left on a board or plate and name the tool that most plausibly did it.

**Rounds:** 6 illustrated or text cases: select the tool capable of the stated action → 2 evidence checks: identify when the result alone cannot prove which tool was used.

**Example task:** Which listed tool is designed to produce adjustable thin slices? Choose from a mandoline, whisk, ladle and rolling pin.

**Result:** Detective rank from raw score out of 8, with a one-line tool explanation per case.

**Audience:** Curious home cooks and gadget-drawer owners; North America, globally playable. **Proposed slug:** `kitchen-tool-detective`.

**Evidence:** [E00](SOURCES.md).

**Production:** Each answer needs a cooking-reference citation and an editor check that no second suspect is equally plausible.
