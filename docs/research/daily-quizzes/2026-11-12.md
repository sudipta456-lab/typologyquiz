- **PC-33**. At a fictional drop-in craft workshop, decide how you would begin a new project and revise it. Nearest actual tasks checked: PC-05, PC-17, PC-20. Deadline prioritization, not how to enter and revise an unfamiliar craft. Ambient sound preference, not a workshop process. Route choice, not demonstration versus trial and solo versus feedback.
- **FS-33**. Rebuild three mismatched fictional swap envelopes from printed records, then handle a missing clue fairly. Nearest actual tasks checked: FS-25, FS-26, FS-32. People-to-item trading, not correcting marked envelopes from records. Lost-and-found ownership and desk logistics, not a seed-swap ledger. Witness reconciliation, not packet-to-sleeve matching with unresolved ownership.
- **SN-33**. Use simplified temperature layers and multiple ground clues to compare snow, sleet and freezing rain. Nearest actual tasks checked: SN-04, SN-23, SN-32. Forecast clue reading, not vertical precipitation phase changes. General predict-reveal science format, not phase-specific winter cases. Surface frost and ice records, not falling snow/sleet/freezing-rain paths.
- **HC-33**. Use three records to tell when a fictional postcard image was captured, when its edition was printed and when the copy was postmarked. Nearest actual tasks checked: HC-29, HC-23, HC-32. Museum Halloween imagery and reproduction interpretation, not three separately dated fictional events. General source type, not date-by-record limits. Chained-library display chronology, not postcard capture, print and mailing evidence.

Whole café atmosphere and menu, not how visitors traverse samples. Taste knowledge and seasoning, not visitor comparison design. Menu-set reasoning, not a tasting route.
