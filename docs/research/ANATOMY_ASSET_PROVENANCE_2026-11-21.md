# Anatomy atlas asset provenance — 2026-11-21

Eight 1200 × 900 WebP reference plates were rendered from 17 unchanged GLB source files in the HuBMAP Consortium's [HRA CCF 3D Reference Object Library](https://github.com/hubmapconsortium/ccf-3d-reference-object-library/tree/f1a3a63f110e27ff0736047d52d04dba5d3087f9), pinned to revision `f1a3a63f110e27ff0736047d52d04dba5d3087f9`. The source and adapted images are credited to HuBMAP Consortium under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). A [public license copy](/anatomy/LICENSE-HRA-CC-BY-4.0.txt) and [attribution](/anatomy/ATTRIBUTION.md) accompany them. TypologyQuiz changed display materials, lighting, orthographic framing, typography and callout leaders; source geometry and GLB bytes were retained.

The machine-readable [provenance](/anatomy/provenance.json) records every pinned source URL, revision, local SHA-256, image SHA-256, camera parameters, surface node, world-space target, projected source anchor and separate letter-badge position. [The asset manifest](/anatomy/manifest.json) carries the same source identities and mappings. All 17 public GLBs were checked against the cached pinned-source downloads by SHA-256. Their GLB length headers match the actual files; all declared buffers are embedded and there are no external image URIs. The eight final WebP hashes and byte sizes were recomputed after composition.

The badges are centered at 12% or 88% of image width and at 318 or 640 source-image pixels vertically. A/C occupy the left slots and B/D the right slots, ordered by their source anchor height. The `assets.ts` clickable centers use those badge coordinates and 6% dimensions. At a 240 × 180 display, the minimum 44 px targets remain fully inside the image and do not overlap. The original surface anchors remain separate in the provenance data; badge centers are not anatomical surface coordinates.

The body image uses a standalone low-detail outline without spatial registration to the organ models. Other plates show selected HRA source surfaces in their original shared male or female organ coordinate frames. They are oblique exterior or segmented surface views, not medical sections. Source meshes do not establish a verified camera-side convention, so the images make no anatomical view-side or left/right camera claim. The kidney plate omits ureter and bladder models. The male reproductive selection omits testes and penis; its node `VH_M_seminal_vesicle` has conflicting deferent-duct label/ontology metadata and is excluded from named callouts. Male B is the explicitly labeled `VH_M_base_of_prostate` mesh. Respiratory A was reprojected to the visible ringed trachea surface and checked with a same-node ray cast.

These are general learning references. They do not claim complete organ coverage, clinical certification, diagnosis or treatment use. This asset verification does not establish browser or full-quiz integration readiness.
