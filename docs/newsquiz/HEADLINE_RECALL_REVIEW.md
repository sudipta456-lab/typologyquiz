# News question difficulty review — September 10, 2026

Status: wording preview and candidate pool only. No publication approval.
The operator reported that the current round feels hard and misses what
people actually remember. The editorial response is to change the question
target, not merely make wrong answers implausible. The operator clarified that a person watching or reading news for 1–2 hours weekly should feel comfortable attempting every question. Original questions 4 (court dissent) and 7 (interest-rate level) are positive examples. Do not make the whole round basic or eliminate numbers categorically.

This is a question-design audit, not a fresh factual endorsement of those
inherited stories. Immutable legacy data has not been rewritten.

## Easier wording preview

1. **Whose portrait appears on Canada's new $20 bill, unveiled on September 3?**
   Viola Desmond / **King Charles III** / Queen Elizabeth II / Wilfrid Laurier.
   The design was unveiled; circulation is planned for late February 2027.
   Takeaway: recognizable change to familiar currency. Intended difficulty:
   easy. [Bank of Canada announcement](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/2026/09/bank-canada-unveils-new-vertical-20-bank-note/).

2. **At what level did the Bank of Canada keep its main interest rate on September 2?**
   1.5% / **2.25%** / 3% / 4.25%.
   Retains the rate-level target the operator specifically called perfect.
   Takeaway: a familiar headline number affecting borrowing costs. Intended
   difficulty: moderate, appropriate for the 1–2-hour weekly reader.
   [Bank of Canada decision](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/2026/09/fad-press-release-2026-09-02/).

3. **Canada's counter-tariffs taking effect on September 8 targeted imports from which country?**
   China / The United Kingdom / **The United States** / Mexico.
   Takeaway: the central parties in a trade dispute. Intended difficulty: easy.
   Source is an August 26 backgrounder for the September 8 effective date;
   publication and event dates must remain distinct.
   [Department of Finance Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/news/2026/08/list-of-products-from-the-united-states-subject-to-counter-tariffs-effective-september-8-2026.html).

4. **What happened to employment in Canada in August, according to the September 4 jobs report?**
   It increased / **It decreased** / It stayed unchanged.
   Takeaway: the direction of employment, rather than the decimal unemployment
   rate. Intended difficulty: moderate. Use as an alternative to another
   economics question, not as mandatory extra financial content.
   [Statistics Canada release](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/daily-quotidien/260904/dq260904a-eng.htm).
