## Positioning and factual boundaries

Position the account as practical self-reflection and enjoyable quizzes, with transparent explanations of what results mean. Lead with Big Five education and the free Mini-IPIP self-report version; link to the public methodology when discussing evidence. Do not describe TypologyQuiz's implementation as independently validated, diagnostic, clinically accurate, or capable of discovering someone's true identity. Engagement measures content performance, not psychometric validity.

Use existing published content only. Label playful personality material as entertainment. Use original examples rather than reproducing CRT/VVIQ items while their rights remain unresolved. Do not reveal private result links, participant answers, names, browser-local reflections, or unpublished news editions. Product demonstrations must use clearly labelled example data. Avoid inviting viewers to disclose sensitive mental-health information in comments.

## Ten daily pieces

Each row is one distinct post, with its own example and payoff. Comments, duplicated uploads and cross-posted copies do not count toward ten.

Initial mix: seven videos and three carousels; seven self-reflection/education pieces, one original trivia piece, one playful piece and one direct product demonstration. Give every post useful standalone value. Use a specific, optional CTA such as “Save the reflection prompt” or “Explore the free Big Five version at typologyquiz.com.” Avoid repeated pressure to comment, tagging strangers, or pretending educational content is independent of the brand.

Production defaults are our test choices: 1080×1920 vertical exports, clear captions, legible phone-sized text, and 12–25 seconds for most videos. Open with a concrete situation or question in the first two seconds, give the payoff, then one CTA. Keep important text away from interface overlays. Use voice, relevant screen capture, or meaningful visual changes rather than ten static text loops. TikTok's advertising creative guidance supports vertical production, safe areas and hook/body/close structure; this is a useful starting point for organic experiments, not proof of organic results. ## Seven-day test: 70 distinct posts

Day numbers begin on the first actual publication day. Do not backdate a launch if setup takes longer. Repeat the ten-slot structure with new examples daily; maintain a 70-row production ledger.

| Day | Main learning question | Controlled change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Which topics and formats earn attention? | Establish baseline across the ten slots; record actual lengths and posting times. |
| 2 | Which opening is clearer? | Compare scenario-first versus question-first openings in matched educational topics. Keep length and CTA similar. |
| 3 | Does concise or fuller explanation work better? | Compare approximately 12–15 versus 20–25 seconds in matched topics. Do not also change the hook style. |
| 4 | What makes explanations easier to follow? | Compare simple illustrated explanations with example screen demonstrations in suitable matched topics. |
| 5 | Which invitation fits the audience? | Compare save-oriented versus optional website CTAs. Track profile visits as well as engagement. |
| 6 | Do promising formats repeat? | Create new examples in the two strongest formats; retain three exploratory slots. Never re-upload an identical winner. |
| 7 | What should continue next week? | Replicate promising topic/format combinations; preserve a baseline comparison and review production cost. |

These are directional comparisons, not randomized causal tests: topic, distribution and audience differ. Do not declare a winner from one outlier or from posts of different ages. Keep day 6–7 conclusions provisional until their 72-hour data arrives.

Use ten provisional local slots in **America/Edmonton**: 09:00, 10:15, 11:30, 12:45, 14:00, 15:15, 16:30, 17:45, 19:00 and 20:15. These are operational spacing choices, not verified best times. Adjust after actual audience-activity data is available; verify the scheduler's displayed timezone on every batch. Do not publish the whole backlog at once after a missed day.

Batch work: research and outline all ten; create in three reusable visual templates; review all claims/captions/audio; export and preview each file; upload and verify each status. Start with a 3–4-hour daily production budget, measure actual time, and improve templates where quality holds. If ten thoughtful pieces cannot be completed, record the shortfall and its cause instead of counting unfinished files as published posts.

## Measurement and daily research

Record post ID/URL, pillar, format, hook, duration, CTA, audio/license, disclosure choice, planned/actual time, publication status and production minutes. Collect metrics at comparable ages (24 hours and 72 hours). Use available native analytics: views, average watch time, completion, likes, comments, shares, saves, profile visits and followers. Mark unavailable fields **unavailable**, not zero. Account-level profile/follower changes must not be presented as exact per-post attribution.

Compare medians by format and topic, average watch time divided by duration where available, shares plus saves per 1,000 views, and production minutes per useful published post. Use profile-visit/view and follower/view ratios only with compatible scopes and date ranges. Track public landing traffic separately; do not send assessment answers, result links or reflection notes to analytics. The current Business Suite documents downloadable analytics and audience activity, but actual account data has not been inspected. [Business Suite analytics](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/navigate-web-business-suite?lang=en).

Daily research checklist, about 30–45 minutes:

1. Read yesterday's available analytics and any genuine audience questions. Compare equal-age posts; note missing data and changes in distribution.
2. Inspect Creative Hub for the relevant market/date window. Record three examples with source URLs and the useful creative pattern, not copied scripts. Its current guide lists 1/7/15/30-day trend windows. [Creative Hub](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/navigate-web-business-suite?lang=en).
3. If available, inspect Creator Search Insights for relevant questions/content gaps. Availability is regional; record observed queries and dates instead of inventing current trends. [Official Search Insights announcement](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/creator-search-insights?lang=en).
4. Select at most two timely ideas that fit the account; use evergreen topics when no relevant trend is verified. Check educational facts and the linked public site page.
5. Check audio rights, own-brand disclosure, AI labels if applicable, privacy, accurate subtitles, and the actual landing-page link. Preview each export on a phone-sized canvas.
6. Produce the ten briefs; update the ledger and record which observation motivates the day's single-variable comparison. Verify publication/scheduling receipts separately from asset creation.
