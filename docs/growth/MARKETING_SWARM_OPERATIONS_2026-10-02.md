# TypologyQuiz marketing swarm

## Objective

Build a repeatable, evidence-led path to at least one qualified opportunity per 24-hour cycle. An opportunity is one of:

- a visitor who deliberately starts a public quiz from a tracked referral;
- a publisher, educator, creator, or community moderator who asks for a resource, reply, or collaboration;
- an explicitly opted-in contact or partnership inquiry.

Anonymous visits, likes, impressions, and generic reactions are not counted as leads or clients.

## Four roles and daily cadence

| Role | Runs | Owns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Market scout | 00:00, 06:00, 12:00, 18:00 | Current questions, publishers, communities, search gaps, and source-checked opportunity cards. |
| Publisher relations | 09:00, 15:00 | Small, individual publisher and educator outreach with a useful, relevant resource. |
| Community contributor | 11:00, 17:00, 23:00 | Helpful public answers in relevant conversations, with transparent affiliation when TypologyQuiz is mentioned. |
| Growth operator | 20:00 | Referrals, conversion signals, content quality, technical discoverability, and the next-day priority. |

All times are America/Edmonton. Each role leaves a dated note under `docs/growth/market-ops/` so the next role can use what was learned.

## Working rules

Every interaction must be specific to the page or discussion, useful without a click, fact-checked where it makes a factual claim, and written in a natural human voice. State affiliation with TypologyQuiz when the service is mentioned. Link to a single matching live route only when it directly helps the reader.

The swarm does not buy traffic, scrape private data, impersonate people, request private details, mass-comment, automate repetitive replies, use fabricated testimonials, offer psychological diagnoses, coordinate engagement, or send unsolicited DMs. It does not make guarantees about personality results, learning outcomes, search rankings, or traffic.

Publisher and community work are deliberately narrow: at most three individually researched publisher contacts and two public contributions in any 24-hour period. Repeated sources, low-relevance topics, political discussions, grief/crisis content, and pages whose rules disallow promotion are excluded. A public reply belongs only where a reasonable reader would welcome the answer.

## Quality gate

Before an external action, the responsible role verifies:

1. The visible account is exactly `@typologyquiz` for social actions, or the approved TypologyQuiz sender for email.
2. The destination route returns a usable live page and matches the discussion.
3. The statement is supported by a primary or credible source when it contains a fact.
4. The copy discloses the affiliation where appropriate and contains no invented popularity or urgency.
5. The interaction, permalink or sent-message receipt, source, destination, and outcome are recorded.

If an account, destination, or platform rule cannot be verified, the role records a draft and moves on. It does not treat a draft as sent, posted, published, or a lead.

## Daily scorecard

The growth operator records only observed counts for:

- qualified opportunities created;
- verified referral visits and quiz starts;
- replies, partnership inquiries, and consented contacts;
- public contributions and targeted publisher messages;
- source pages checked, conversion issues fixed, and blocked actions.

The target is a disciplined operating goal, not a promise. The operator reviews a seven-day trend before changing volume or tactics.
