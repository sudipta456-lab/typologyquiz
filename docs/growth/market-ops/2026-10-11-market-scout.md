# Market intelligence check — 2026-10-11

## Opportunity card: map-based geography warm-up for educators

**Rank: medium-high relevance; qualified educator fit, no external contact identified**

- **Audience:** educators and librarians looking for a short, social geography activity to pair with map work.
- **Exact source URLs:** [National Geographic Education’s September 23 update on MapMaker guided lessons](https://blog.education.nationalgeographic.org/2026/09/23/mapping-the-future-together-esri-and-the-national-geographic-society/) describes new geography collections, including World Cultures and Geography. Its [Geography-Rich Classroom resource](https://education.nationalgeographic.org/resource/geography-rich-classroom/) recommends coordinate games, map discussion, and student-created maps.
- **Why this audience may care:** these sources show a current, educator-facing use case for brief geography activities that lead into map discussion. They do not endorse TypologyQuiz or establish that a quiz improves learning.
- **Matching verified live TypologyQuiz routes:** `https://typologyquiz.com/trivia/countries-of-europe/` and `https://typologyquiz.com/live/` both returned HTTP 200 on October 11. The first is a self-paced country-capital warm-up; the second offers a facilitator-led shared session.
- **Human-specific helpful angle:** offer a plain 10-minute routine: teams make a choice, point to the country on a map, reveal the answer, then spend one minute explaining a location clue. Treat the quiz as an optional conversation starter, not an assessment or replacement for map work.
- **Factual sources:** National Geographic Education pages above; the route checks recorded above.
- **Safe next step:** prepare a one-page, accessible “map warm-up” facilitator card with the exact activity flow and source links. Publisher relations may consider a single individualized outreach only after identifying a page that explicitly accepts educator resource suggestions.
