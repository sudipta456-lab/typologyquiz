# Opportunity card — astroEDU astronomy activity submission — 2026-10-06

## Rank: high audience fit, substantial preparation required

- **Audience:** astronomy educators seeking peer-reviewed, classroom-ready activities.
- **Exact source and invitation:** [astroEDU’s FAQ](https://astroedu.iau.org/faq/) says it welcomes astronomy educational activities from educators, teachers, students, and astronomers, and that submissions go through peer review before publication. Its [activity preparation guidance](https://astroedu.iau.org/guidelines_old/) asks authors to define audience, goals, objectives, activity design, accessibility for mixed abilities, and evaluation.
- **Why this audience may care:** a properly developed, teacher-led Night Sky group activity could give educators a short planetary-recall conversation starter. The existing live quiz alone is not a lesson plan, has not been classroom-tested, and should not be submitted as one.
- **Matching verified live route:** https://typologyquiz.com/live/ returned HTTP 200 on October 6. The host may choose the Night Sky built-in session; https://typologyquiz.com/trivia/planets/ is the matching self-paced route.
- **Human-specific helpful angle:** create a small facilitator guide with a 10-minute run-of-show, a no-device/team option, a discussion prompt after each answer, plain-language accessibility alternatives, an age/scope note, and links to primary astronomy sources. Call it an optional general-knowledge activity, never a validated learning assessment.
- **Factual sources:** astroEDU FAQ and activity guidance above. They establish the submission mechanism and content expectations; they do not endorse TypologyQuiz.
- **Safe next step:** build and independently review the facilitator guide before considering a submission. The guide needs original learning objectives, a rights statement for every included asset, and a truthful evaluation plan. Do not submit the existing quiz or claim peer review, classroom testing, or affiliation with the IAU.
