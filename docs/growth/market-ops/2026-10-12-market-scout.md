# Market intelligence check — 2026-10-12

## Opportunity card: Alberta Class 7 study-plan resource

**Rank: medium-high relevance; a direct learner need, but no named publisher or opted-in contact identified.**

- **Audience:** Alberta Class 7 learners and learner-support educators seeking structured, accessible practice before the official knowledge test.
- **Exact source URL:** [Alberta driver’s knowledge test](https://www.alberta.ca/drivers-knowledge-test). The province says the test has 30 multiple-choice questions, requires 25 correct to pass, may be taken once per day, and provides audio, oral, and language support options. Its [driver guides overview](https://www.alberta.ca/driver-guides-overview-and-pdf-versions) says study and practice support safe driving and links the official guide.
- **Why this audience may care:** the official test page establishes a concrete preparation need and accessibility context. It does not recommend TypologyQuiz or validate any third-party question bank.
- **Matching verified live TypologyQuiz route:** `https://typologyquiz.com/driving/alberta/` returned HTTP 200 on October 12. The public `llms.txt` describes six Alberta practice sets and source-linked handbook excerpts; the route must continue to identify itself as practice rather than the official test.
- **Human-specific helpful angle:** offer an optional, non-diagnostic 15-minute routine: read the matching official-guide section, do one focused practice set, then explain each missed answer in plain language. Direct learners to the provincial page for the official test, languages, and accommodations.
- **Factual sources:** Alberta pages above; route check recorded above.
- **Safe next step:** create a compact, accessible “Class 7 study routine” page or facilitator card that links to the official Alberta pages and the matching live practice route. Publisher relations should use it only with an identified, opt-in resource-submission channel; do not imply that it is official preparation material or that it improves a pass rate.

## Opportunity card: map-language warm-up that leads to discussion

**Rank: medium relevance; educator fit, no verified submission channel.**

- **Audience:** early-years educators and librarians using classroom maps or map-skills activities.
- **Exact source URL:** [National Geographic Education’s Mapping the Classroom](https://education.nationalgeographic.org/resource/mapping-the-classroom/) asks learners to use location language such as *inside*, *next to*, *between*, *near*, and *far*. The related [Geography-Rich Classroom](https://education.nationalgeographic.org/resource/geography-rich-classroom/) suggests map-based games and follow-up discussion.
- **Why this audience may care:** this is a compatible use for a short, optional geography prompt followed by map-based explanation. Neither National Geographic page endorses TypologyQuiz or shows demand for a new resource.
- **Matching verified live TypologyQuiz routes:** `https://typologyquiz.com/trivia/countries-of-the-world/` and `https://typologyquiz.com/live/` returned HTTP 200 on October 12.
- **Human-specific helpful angle:** let pairs choose an answer, find the location on a map, then say one relationship such as “north of,” “near,” or “across from.” The point is geographic language and map use, not the quiz score.
- **Factual sources:** National Geographic Education pages above; route checks recorded above.
- **Safe next step:** the existing proposed map-warm-up facilitator card remains the highest-quality collateral. Do not approach the cited publisher unless a current, explicit resource-submission channel is identified.
