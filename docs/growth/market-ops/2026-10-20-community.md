### 1. https://www.reddit.com/r/APHumanGeography/comments/1x8tu8n/comment/phluo0n/

Unchanged visible copy:

Get a blank copy of the map your class uses. Pick one small region, cover the labels and try to place or name the countries from memory. Check against the answer key and note the ones you mixed up. After a break, try those again on a blank map; tomorrow, mix them back with the others. Also practise the test's direction: placing a country from its name is a different task from naming a highlighted country. A game is fine, but use one that matches what you'll actually have to do.

### 2. https://www.reddit.com/r/APStudents/comments/1x99ekz/comment/phqa3aq/

Unchanged visible copy:

For graph/map MCQs, try doing a quick read of the figure before looking at the options: what do the axes or map key measure, which place/time is shown, and what pattern can you actually point to? Then read the question and name the course concept it asks you to apply. When you check an answer, label the miss as 'misread the figure', 'didn't know the concept', or 'couldn't apply it'. Bring one missed question and that label to your teacher, and ask them to walk through the reasoning with you. That gives you a specific thing to work on in the next practice set.
