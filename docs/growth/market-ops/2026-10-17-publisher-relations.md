**Exact submitted listing description:**

Submitted by Sudipta Sarkar on behalf of TypologyQuiz for SciTech Institute’s Physical Sciences and Periodic Table of Elements resource collection.

This free browser activity asks learners to recall the first 20 element names, hydrogen through calcium, in three minutes. Optional symbol hints are available, and names can be entered in any order. It is intended as an informal recall warm-up, not a formal assessment.

Suggested nine-minute facilitator sequence:
1. Recall (three minutes): use the quiz in pairs, or write Si, Ca, O and C on paper and ask for their element names. Continue with other first-20 elements.
2. Explain (three minutes): compare answers and ask each pair to explain one symbol/name match. Paper key: Si = silicon; Ca = calcium; O = oxygen; C = carbon.
3. Discuss (three minutes): choose a familiar material and discuss why recalling an element’s name alone does not explain the material’s properties. Check explanations against a trusted chemistry reference.

Allow untimed paper answers and spoken or written explanations when speed or typing is a barrier. Suggested for learners already familiar with element symbols; the facilitator should review suitability. This suggested sequence has not been classroom-tested, and no standards alignment or improvement in learning is claimed.
